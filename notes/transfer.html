Soccer Transfer Value Model

Python · ML · in progress · 3 min read

The question

What is a Premier League forward actually worth? Transfermarkt publishes market values for every player. Can a model get close to them from stats alone?

Why it's useful

Clubs spend hundreds of millions on players. A stats-based estimate gives a second opinion on whether a fee is too high or a bargain.

What I did

  • Collected 547 Premier League players: stats from FBref, plus wages, contracts and full value history from Transfermarkt.
  • Trained a blend of LightGBM and ridge regression on every position, then scored it on the 153 forwards and wingers.
  • Compared it with the simplest guess: the player's previous valuation.

What I found

The model's typical miss is 12.3% of a player's value, against 18.2% for just using the last valuation. 82% of its estimates land within 25% of the real value.

What surprised me

How much a player's past valuations matter. Just using the last valuation already gets within about 18% on average. The stats bring that down to about 12%.

What I'd do next

Score it on midfielders and defenders too, not just forwards and wingers.
